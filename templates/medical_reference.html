Essential Medical Reference Values

Normal ranges every person should know. Always consult a doctor for personal medical advice.

Vital Signs
Blood Pressure
120/80 mmHg
Systolic / Diastolic. Above 140/90 is hypertension.
Heart Rate
60 – 100 bpm
Resting heart rate. Below 60 is bradycardia, above 100 is tachycardia.
Body Temperature
97°F – 99°F
Normal: 36.1°C to 37.2°C. Above 38°C is fever.
Respiratory Rate
12 – 20 /min
Breaths per minute at rest. Children: 14–24/min.
Blood Volume
5 – 6 Litres
Average total blood volume in the human body.
Oxygen Saturation
95 – 100%
SpO2 level. Below 92% requires medical attention.
Blood Sugar & Cholesterol
Fasting Blood Sugar
70 – 100 mg/dl
Normal fasting glucose. 100–125 is pre-diabetes. Above 126 is diabetes.
Post-meal Blood Sugar
70 – 140 mg/dl
2 hours after eating. Above 200 mg/dl suggests diabetes.
Total Cholesterol
Below 200 mg/dl
130–200 is desirable. 200–239 borderline. Above 240 is high.
HDL (Good) Cholesterol
Above 40 mg/dl
Higher is better. Above 60 is protective against heart disease.
LDL (Bad) Cholesterol
Below 100 mg/dl
Lower is better. Above 160 is high risk for heart disease.
Triglycerides
Below 150 mg/dl
Blood fats. 150–199 is borderline. Above 200 is high.
Blood Count
Red Blood Cells
4.50 – 6.0 million/µL
Male: 4.7–6.1, Female: 4.2–5.4 million cells per microlitre.
White Blood Cells
4,000 – 11,000 /µL
Immune cells. High count may indicate infection or inflammation.
Platelets
150,000 – 400,000 /µL
Clotting cells. Low count causes bleeding risk.
Haemoglobin
Male: 13–18 g/dl
Female: 11.5–16 g/dl. Carries oxygen in red blood cells.
Iron
8 – 15 mg/day
Daily requirement. Low iron causes anaemia and fatigue.
HbA1c
Below 5.7%
3-month average blood sugar. 5.7–6.4% pre-diabetes. Above 6.5% diabetes.
Minerals & Vitamins
Calcium
8.6 – 10.3 mg/dl
Essential for bones, teeth and heart function.
Potassium
3.5 – 5.0 mEq/L
Vital for heart rhythm and muscle function.
Sodium
135 – 145 mEq/L
Controls fluid balance. High sodium raises blood pressure.
Vitamin D3
20 – 50 ng/ml
Below 20 is deficiency. Essential for bones and immunity.
Vitamin B12
200 – 900 pg/ml
Below 200 causes nerve damage and anaemia.
Magnesium
1.7 – 2.2 mg/dl
Needed for muscle, nerve and heart function.
Disclaimer: These are general reference ranges for adults. Normal values may vary by lab, age, sex and health condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for interpretation.
